Questions brands ask

What is Garrab?

Garrab is AI virtual try-on for online brands. Your shoppers upload a photo and see your clothing on themselves, or your home décor piece in their own room, before they buy.

Does it work with Shopify, WooCommerce or Instagram?

Yes. On Shopify, WooCommerce or any website, a Try it on button appears under Add to cart on your approved products: one line of code, which we install for you. Brands without a website use a Garrab try-on link in their Instagram bio, stories and WhatsApp.

How much does it cost?

The first 14 days are free: 300 previews, all your products, no card. Then Starter is 5,000 EGP a month with 250 previews, and Growth is 8,500 EGP a month with 600 previews and the size finder. Extra previews are 800 EGP per 100.

Does it work for home décor?

Yes. Shoppers upload a photo of their room and see your lamp, rug, cushion, art or mirror placed in it.

Is it in Arabic?

Yes. The try-on pages work in Arabic, right to left, and in English, and the size finder answers in both.

What happens to the shopper's photo?

It's used only to create that shopper's preview. Garrab doesn't store it, and hidden details such as location are removed before it leaves the phone.

How accurate are the previews?

Previews are AI images: a close guide to how a piece looks, not an exact photo. Every product is tested first, and you approve each one before shoppers can try it.

How long does setup take?

Usually the same day. We read your products and test them, and you approve the ones you like. Your try-on link works as soon as one product is approved.
