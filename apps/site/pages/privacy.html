Privacy in plain words

Shoppers

When you try a product on, your photo is used only to create your preview. We send it to our try-on provider, show you the result, and ask the provider to delete it straight away. We do not keep your photo, the preview, your measurements or your size answers in our databases, logs or analytics. We don't record your IP address.

Before your photo leaves your phone or computer, we remove its hidden details, such as location and device information.

Our provider: previews (your photo, or a photo of your room for home décor shops) and the size finder use Google's Gemini API (paid service, set not to store requests). Google does not use your photo to train or improve its products, but keeps requests for a limited time to detect abuse. That is why we say we don't save your photo on our systems: we never store it, but Google may keep it briefly for safety checks.

We count anonymous totals (for example "120 button views today") per shop. Nothing in them identifies you. To count how many people used try-on, your browser keeps one thing on your own device: the date of your last preview for that shop. We only learn "this is a first preview today" (yes or no), never who you are.

Brands

We keep your business details (name, email, WhatsApp number, website, products and invoices) to run the service. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Garrab's own website and advertising

On garrabb.com and on our sign-up page (app.garrabb.com/start) we use the Meta Pixel to measure our own adverts: page visits, taps on our WhatsApp and sign-up buttons, and completed sign-ups. Meta may set cookies for this. When a brand signs up, we also tell Meta about the sign-up from our server, with the email and WhatsApp number in a scrambled (hashed) form, so Meta can match it to our advert. This is only about Garrab's own marketing to brands.

The Meta Pixel is never used on shops' try-on pages, shoppers' photos or previews, the brand portal or payment pages.
