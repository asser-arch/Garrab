Privacy in plain words

Shoppers

When you try a product on, your photo is used only to create your preview. We send it to our try-on provider, show you the result, and ask the provider to delete it straight away. We do not keep your photo, the preview, your measurements or your size answers in our databases, logs or analytics. We don't record your IP address.

Before your photo leaves your phone or computer, we remove its hidden details, such as location and device information.

Our providers: clothing and accessory previews use Genlook (France), our subprocessor under its data processing agreement, which deletes photos when we ask and keeps nothing longer than its retention window (set to 1 day). Room previews and the size finder use Google's Gemini API (paid service): Google does not use them to train or improve its products, but keeps requests for a limited time to detect abuse.

We count anonymous totals (for example "120 button views today") per shop. Nothing in them identifies you.

Brands

We keep your business details (name, email, WhatsApp number, website, products and invoices) to run the service. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any Garrab email.
